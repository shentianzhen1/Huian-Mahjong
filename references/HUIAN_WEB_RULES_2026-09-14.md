Goal: find public, attributable rules for the target 开心泉州麻将 / 惠安二人房.

## Findings

   generic level.  It says qualifying players may Chi/Peng/Kong, which is weak
   external corroboration for the player's verified observation that target
   two-player Huian can Chi.  It does not establish a two-player room's order,
   gold, flow, or settlement rule.

   multipliers.  It is a different product and has no demonstrated connection
   to the target 开心惠安 two-player room.  Its values are comparison leads only.

## Decisions

- No public source found in this pass can promote a target-room UNKNOWN rule.
- Do not adopt the different product's score or multiplier formula.
- The current high-priority evidence remains: target in-game pages, recorded
  target settlements, and player gameplay feedback.
- The online search's generic Chi statement does not override, but is
  consistent with, confirmed target player feedback that Chi is allowed.
